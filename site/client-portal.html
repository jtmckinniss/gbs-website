What you can do

Everything you need,
in one place.

Complete your intake form

Before your first appointment, complete your dog's intake form directly in the portal. It's thorough — and it's what allows us to make the most of every session.

Request appointments

Request follow-up sessions for Training, Modification, or Medicine — based on your existing care plan. New to Gigi's Behavior? Start with a Care Navigation Call.

View upcoming appointments

See what's scheduled, when, and with which specialist. Add to your calendar directly from the portal.

Manage medications

For families in the Behavioral Medicine program: track your dog's current medications, review dosing instructions, and coordinate refills with your care team.

Current clients

Already working with us?

If you're a current Gigi's Behavior client, the portal is how you stay connected between sessions. Your care team updates your records after every appointment. You can request follow-up sessions, review your dog's care plan, and communicate with your team.

Training clients

Schedule your next class directly through the portal or through the Offering Tree. Your trainer's notes are in your portal after each session.

Modification and Medicine clients

Request follow-up sessions through the portal. Your consultant or Behavioral Medicine Tech reviews requests and confirms scheduling. Emergency or urgent concerns? Text us.

Can't access your portal? Text 937-826-3119 and we'll help you get in.
