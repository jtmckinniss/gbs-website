Urgent behavior concern

Urgent behavioral concerns.

For current Gigi's Behavior clients experiencing an urgent behavioral change or escalation.

When to use this form

  • Biting or attacking a household member or another animal, resulting in significant injury
  • Aggression toward children living in the home
  • Animal-to-animal aggression you cannot safely interrupt or manage
  • Serious self-injury (for example self-biting, tail chasing with trauma, or breaking out of crates and injuring themselves)
  • Increased aggression after starting a new behavioral medication
  • A concerning medication side effect that does not require immediate emergency care

If you're unsure whether your situation needs emergency veterinary care, err on the side of caution and seek in-person medical care first.

How triage works

  • Our team reviews urgent submissions during business hours, Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m.–4:00 p.m., and prioritizes concerns by clinical urgency.
  • If your submission appears urgent, we will make reasonable efforts to respond as soon as possible during business hours. Non-urgent concerns may receive a response within several business days.
  • Submitting this form does not guarantee immediate contact or intervention.
  • If your situation involves active aggression, immediate safety risk or conditions you cannot safely manage, contact your primary care veterinarian or a local emergency veterinary hospital.

Not for medical emergencies.

Seek immediate in-person veterinary care, and do not wait for a response from our team, if your animal is experiencing:

  • Collapse
  • Seizures
  • Uncontrolled bleeding
  • Severe vomiting or diarrhea
  • Signs of medication toxicity
  • Extreme agitation or disorientation
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Any life-threatening condition

Before you submit, please make sure

  • All people and animals are physically separated and safe.
  • You are not actively in a dangerous situation.
  • You understand this is a triage communication tool, not an emergency response.

Acknowledgement. By submitting, you understand this form is not monitored in real time and is not a substitute for emergency care, and you agree to seek immediate in-person medical or veterinary attention if your animal or any person is in danger.
