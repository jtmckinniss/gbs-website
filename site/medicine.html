What to know

What to expect.

A vet referral from your current vet

A referral from your primary veterinarian is required before scheduling. Gigi's collaborates with your vet throughout care. Your primary vet remains the prescribing partner after each telemedicine visit.

Telehealth-first care

All Behavioral Medicine appointments are virtual. Ohio regulations limit telemedicine prescriptions to 14 days per visit. Your primary vet handles prescription continuation. Gigi's manages the behavior case throughout.

Medicine and Modification together

Medicine creates the conditions for learning. Modification is where lasting progress happens. For most dogs, both are part of the plan.

Progress in months, not days

Real impact is measured over months. You'll have regular check-ins, an evolving plan, and a team that's with you for the long term.
