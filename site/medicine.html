What to know

What to expect.

A vet referral from your current vet

A referral from your primary veterinarian is required for Behavioral Medicine. Gigi's collaborates with your vet throughout care.

Telehealth-first care

Dr. Gilbert-Gregory, Lead Veterinary Behaviorist, leads this specialty care. All telehealth appointments are virtual visits. Gigi's manages the behavior case throughout.

Medicine and Modification together

Medicine creates the conditions for learning. Modification is where lasting progress happens. For most dogs, both are part of the plan.

Progress in months, not days

Real impact is measured over months. You'll have regular check-ins, an evolving plan, and a team that's with you for the long term.

Before you begin

Before you schedule.

01

A vet referral

A referral from your primary veterinarian is required. Families may contact Gigi's before obtaining a referral; with their authorization, Gigi's requests it directly from the primary veterinary practice.

02

The Care Navigation Call

The Care Navigation Call is the only entry into Behavioral Medicine. Our Client Success Manager will review your dog's history, confirm the referral, and schedule your Initial Assessment.

On scheduling: Families with a referral in hand are scheduled for the first available appointment. Families with a referral outstanding are scheduled with a 2-week buffer to allow admin time before the appointment.
