Diplomate ACVB Board-Certified Veterinary Behaviorists

Led by board-certified Veterinary Behaviorists. Fewer than 100 practice in the United States.

Board-certified Veterinary Behaviorists complete years of specialized training in animal behavior, learning theory, and behavioral pharmacology. They diagnose behavioral conditions, build treatment plans, and recommend medication when it may help your animal learn and make progress in Modification.

“Medication is a tool, not a setback. It creates the conditions for learning. It doesn't replace the work of Modification.”
