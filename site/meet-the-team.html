Diplomate ACVB Board-Certified Veterinary Behaviorists

Led by board-certified Veterinary Behaviorists. Fewer than 100 practice in the United States.

Board-certified Veterinary Behaviorists complete years of specialized training in animal behavior, learning theory, and behavioral pharmacology. They diagnose behavioral conditions, develop integrated treatment plans, and prescribe medication when it can help the brain reach a place where learning is possible.

“Medication is a tool, not a setback. It creates the conditions for learning. It doesn't replace the work of Modification.”
