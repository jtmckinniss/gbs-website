"Think of it as your dog's therapist."

Modification isn't about correcting behavior — it's about changing the underlying emotional response. With the right support, dogs can learn new ways to feel safe, respond calmly, and build resilience over time.

If your dog struggles, you're not alone.

Most dogs need behavioral support at some point. It's the norm, not the exception. At Gigi's Behavior, there's no judgement, only progress.

HOW CONSULTANT-LED MODIFICATION WORKS

Guided. Measurable. Yours to keep.

Medicine-Led families skip the Starting Point and go straight to sessions with their Behavioral Medicine Tech.

  1. 1

    Book a call

    Your free Care Navigation Call starts the process. Our Client Success Manager reviews your dog's behavior history, answers your questions, and guides you to the right modification pathway.

  2. 2

    The Modification Starting Point

    Your first appointment is a 90-minute virtual session with your Behavior Consultant. You'll share history, set goals, and leave with a clear behavior modification plan.

  3. 3

    Consistent practice

    Modification happens between sessions. Your consultant gives you the tools — you do the daily work. Progress is built through repetition, not just appointments.

  4. 4

    Ongoing support

    Follow-up sessions adjust the plan as your dog progresses. Virtual, in-person at Gigi's, or in-home for behaviorally required cases at Gigi's discretion.
