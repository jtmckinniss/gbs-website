The collaboration model

How we work together.

When you refer a patient to Gigi's, you remain the prescribing partner. Ohio telemedicine regulations limit prescription authority to 14 days per telemedicine visit — after each visit, prescription continuation transitions to you. Gigi's manages the behavioral case, develops the treatment plan, and keeps you informed throughout. Your client should feel supported by a team, not handed off.

Prescriptions
You prescribe, we manage the case
We collaborate with you on the prescription transition. You continue the medication your client's animal is already on; we continue managing the behavior plan.
Boundaries
Joint care, clear boundaries
We do not expect you to provide behavior counseling or modification. That stays with us. You handle prescription continuation; we handle everything else.
Communication
Communication throughout
We provide clinical updates on your referred patients and are available for vet-to-vet consults at no charge for the transition call.

Ohio regulations

Ohio telemedicine — what this means for you.

Under Ohio regulations, telemedicine prescriptions require a new VCPR visit every 14 days. This means prescription continuation after each visit transitions to you, the primary veterinarian. Here's how it works in practice:

01
Gigi's veterinary behaviorist prescribes at the initial assessment and at each subsequent virtual visit where the VCPR is re-established.
02
After 14 days, prescription continuation is your responsibility. Gigi's will coordinate the transition and provide the medication name, dose, and clinical context.
03
You are not responsible for behavioral counseling, modification, or case management — that stays entirely with Gigi's. You're continuing a prescription already in progress.
Key principle

Your client should never feel handed off. Gigi's manages the relationship and the behavior case. You handle prescription continuation.
