Specialty: Medicine

Choose how we collaborate.

Three ways to work with Gigi's on Behavioral Medicine. All three require a full veterinary referral.

Featured

Gigi's-Managed Behavioral Medicine

Refer your patient to Gigi's for specialty behavioral treatment and behavioral medication management. Our Veterinary Behaviorist leads the behavioral plan and ongoing progress, while our on-site veterinarian independently manages prescriptions following an appropriate in-person examination and valid VCPR. Your practice has no routine behavioral prescribing or follow-up responsibilities. You may review available notes, discharges and medication updates in the referral portal if you choose.

Alternative

Vet-Partnered Behavioral Medicine

We manage the behavioral treatment. Your practice manages prescriptions when needed. Gigi's works directly with your client to assess their dog, develop the care plan and monitor behavioral progress, while collaborating closely with your practice. If medication is recommended, you maintain the prescribing VCPR and independently review and authorize recommended medications, dose changes and refills. Gigi's does not hold the prescribing VCPR or issue prescriptions under this model. This option is intended for families unable to travel to Gigi's or practices that want to retain prescribing authority. Timely collaboration is essential to prevent treatment delays.

Clinician-only

Vet-to-Vet Consultation

Consult directly with our Veterinary Behaviorist about your patient's behavioral concerns and possible treatment approaches. Gigi's does not communicate with the family, establish a patient relationship or deliver ongoing case management through this service. Your practice remains responsible for clinical decisions, prescriptions and follow-up.

What to expect

The referral process.

01

You submit a referral

Use the referral form below, or call us directly. Include the patient's history, presenting complaint, and any current medications.

02

We schedule the client

Clients with your referral in hand are scheduled at first available. Clients who haven't yet obtained a referral are scheduled with a 2-week buffer.

03

Initial Behavioral Medicine Assessment

A virtual appointment with our Veterinary Behaviorist. We complete a comprehensive behavioral evaluation, develop a diagnosis and treatment plan, and, where clinically appropriate, recommend medication.

04

We keep you in the loop

You'll receive a summary after the initial assessment.
