How it works

Two formats. One care team.

Whether you're looking for the energy and structure of a group class or the focused attention of a 1-on-1 session, both formats are led by the same trainers and connect to the same advanced care team when needed.

Group Training

Small groups, by design.

Group classes give your dog structured practice in a controlled, low-stress environment while you build skills alongside other families. Puppy Preschool runs up to 8 puppies; Skills & Manners runs up to 6 dogs.

Puppy Preschool

Your puppy's first structured socialization experience. Foundational skills, early confidence building, and positive first exposures to people, environments, and other dogs.

Skills & Manners // Foundations

A 6-week series. The starting point for foundational obedience and manners work.

Skills & Manners // Advanced

A 6-week series. Builds on Foundations with more complex cues and real-world generalization.

Tricks Class // Drop In

Enrichment-focused skill building in a fun, low-pressure setting. Tricks work builds confidence, focus, and the family-dog communication that underpins everything else. Drop-in format.

1-on-1 Training

1-on-1, focused on you.

1-on-1 sessions give you undivided time with a trainer. They follow the same Skills & Manners curriculum as our group classes, with just you, your dog, and your trainer.

Skills & Manners // Foundations (1-on-1)

The Skills & Manners Foundations curriculum, delivered 1-on-1. A good fit when your household schedule doesn't align with group classes.

Skills & Manners // Advanced (1-on-1)

The Skills & Manners Advanced curriculum, delivered 1-on-1.
