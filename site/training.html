How it works

Two formats. Choose what fits your dog.

Some dogs thrive on the energy of a group. Others focus better 1-on-1, or your schedule needs more flexibility. Both formats teach the same skills with the same credentialed trainers, so you can pick what works for your dog and your life. Every class and every 1-on-1 session takes place in person at Gigi's.

Group Training

Small groups, by design.

Group classes give your dog structured practice in a calm, controlled setting while you build skills alongside other families. Puppy Preschool is capped at 8 puppies; Skills & Manners at 6 dogs.

small on purpose

Puppy Preschool

Open to puppies under 20 weeks old. A 4-class series you can join any week. Your puppy's first structured socialization experience. Foundational skills, early confidence building, and positive first exposures to people, environments, and other dogs.

Skills & Manners // Foundations

A 6-week series. The starting point for foundational skills and good manners (what most people call obedience).

Skills & Manners // Advanced

A 6-week series. Builds on Foundations with more complex cues and real-world generalization.

Tricks Classes // 4-Class Series

A new trick each week for four weeks. Drop in for any class, or save by booking all four. Builds confidence and focus, and gives your dog skills you can really show off!

1-on-1, focused on you.

1-on-1 sessions give you undivided time with a trainer, in person at Gigi's. They follow the same Skills & Manners curriculum as our group classes, with just you, your dog, and your trainer.
